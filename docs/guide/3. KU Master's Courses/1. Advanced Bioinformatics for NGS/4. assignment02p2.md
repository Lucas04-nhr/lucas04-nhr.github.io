## Methods {#methods}

I extracted the ±250 kb region around the platelet-count lead variant rs6425521 from `platelet_astle.ma` (hg19), calculated phased reference-allele-oriented LD in the 1000 Genomes European panel with PLINK2, aligned the GWAS effect alleles to the LD matrix, and ran `susie_rss` with the median GWAS sample size (`N = 166,066`). Coordinates were converted to hg38 with the supplied UCSC chain file. Functional interpretation was restricted to credible-set variants with PIP > 0.1 and used Ensembl VEP, the ENCODE Registry of candidate cis-regulatory elements presented through SCREEN, and the supplied AlphaGenome AVI scores. All resource checks were performed on 24 September 2026.

::: warning LD Consistency
These PIPs require caution. The LD matrix was not positive semidefinite (minimum eigenvalue −2.57), and the `kriging_rss` diagnostic found 143 variants with an absolute standardized discrepancy of at least 2. In particular, the very high PIPs assigned to variants with modest or null marginal GWAS evidence in CS2 and CS3 are not biologically credible without LD/reference-panel sensitivity analyses. The most internally consistent statistical signal is CS1, which contains the lead association peak.
:::

## Functional Evidence from VEP, SCREEN, and AlphaGenome {#functional-evidence}

All seven candidates lie within **DNM3**. [Ensembl VEP](https://www.ensembl.org/Tools/VEP) classifies rs2038479 as a 5-prime UTR variant and the other six candidates as intronic variants. These are non-coding annotations and do not alone establish a regulatory effect.

The hg38 [ENCODE cCRE track](https://api.genome.ucsc.edu/getData/track?genome=hg38&track=encodeCcreCombined&chrom=chr1&start=171900000&end=171990000), which contains the registry displayed by [SCREEN](https://screen.wenglab.org/), shows that rs2038479 directly overlaps **EH38E1397407**, a distal enhancer-like signature (dELS; chr1:171969898-171970203 in one-based coordinates). None of the other six PIP > 0.1 candidates directly overlaps a registry cCRE. A lack of direct overlap does not exclude cell-type-specific regulation, but rs2038479 receives the clearest chromatin-based support.

Four candidates occur in the supplied AlphaGenome table:

::: table title="AlphaGenome AVI Scores for Scored Candidates" align="center" copy="all"
| Variant | AVI raw | AVI PHRED | Interpretation relative to the background distribution |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| rs2038479 | 0.1865 | 11.053 | High; above the background 95th percentile (10.188) |
| rs2208368 | 0.1372 | 9.430 | Strong but just below the predefined high-AVI threshold |
| rs2145380 | 0.0218 | 4.444 | Modest |
| rs2038480 | −0.0275 | 1.489 | Low |
:::

The supplied file contains no AVI rows for rs6425521, rs2093184, or rs12145667, so no AlphaGenome effect is claimed for them. Direct Atlas links for the scored candidates are [rs2038479](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr1%3A171970150%3AC%3EA&m=variant), [rs2038480](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr1%3A171970501%3AA%3ET&m=variant), [rs2145380](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr1%3A171915846%3AG%3EC&m=variant), and [rs2208368](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr1%3A171923265%3AA%3EG&m=variant). The AVI score is a prioritisation score rather than proof of the affected molecular track or cell type.

The independent sources agree most clearly for rs2038479: it is in the lead statistical credible set, has a regulatory VEP consequence, overlaps a dELS, and has high predicted impact. This conclusion is also consistent with prior experimental work identifying rs2038479 at a MEIS1/RUNX1 binding site near an alternative DNM3 promoter in megakaryocytes ([Nürnberg et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22972982/)). The low AVI score for rs2038480 despite nearly identical marginal GWAS significance illustrates how LD can produce strong statistical association without equally strong predicted sequence effects.

## Candidate Mediating Cell Types {#mediating-cell-types}

The most plausible mediating cells are **megakaryocytes and their hematopoietic progenitors**, the bone-marrow lineage that produces platelets. DNM3 expression increases during megakaryocyte development, and experimental perturbation supports a role in megakaryocyte growth, cytoskeletal remodelling, and proplatelet formation ([Reems et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19007685/)). The rs2038479 study further implicates MEIS1/RUNX1-dependent regulation in megakaryocytes. Platelets themselves are anucleate, so a DNA-regulatory mechanism is more plausibly established earlier in megakaryocyte differentiation than in circulating platelets.

## Variants Prioritised for Follow-up {#prioritised-variants}

1. **rs2038479** is the primary candidate. It combines a substantial CS1 PIP (0.285), extreme marginal association, a 5-prime UTR annotation, direct dELS overlap, high AVI PHRED score, and prior megakaryocyte-specific functional evidence.
2. **rs6425521** is the strongest statistical comparator. It is the GWAS lead variant and has the largest CS1 PIP (0.305), but currently lacks equally specific functional support in the available SCREEN and AlphaGenome results.
3. **rs2038480** should be retained as a negative or LD-matched experimental comparator: it shares the association peak but has low AVI, making it useful for discriminating a causal sequence effect from tagging by LD.

The CS2 and CS3 variants should not be prioritised solely from their fitted PIPs because their marginal GWAS statistics and the LD-consistency diagnostic conflict with those PIPs. Before functional experiments, I would repeat fine-mapping with an ancestry- and cohort-matched LD panel or individual-level genotypes, then test rs2038479 and rs6425521 with allele-specific reporter assays and CRISPR perturbation in CD34-derived megakaryocytes.
